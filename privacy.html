Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Short version: Elyon AI collects only what is necessary to provide the service. We do not sell your data, do not use it to train AI models, and do not share it with third parties except as required for the service to function.

1. Who we are

Elyon AI is an AI assistant service operated by an independent developer. The service is accessible via the web at elyon-ai-web.vercel.app and via the Telegram bot @Elyon_by_unkony_bot.

For questions regarding this policy, contact us via Telegram: @unkonyy

2. What data we collect

Account information

When you sign in, we collect:

  • Your Telegram user ID, username, and first name (when signing in via Telegram)
  • Your email address (when signing in via Google or email/password)
  • Your profile picture (optional, from your auth provider)

Usage data

  • Messages you send to the AI (stored to maintain conversation history)
  • Files you upload for analysis (processed in real time, not stored permanently)
  • Subscription status and payment records
  • Daily message count for rate limiting purposes

Technical data

We do not collect IP addresses, device fingerprints, or browser metadata beyond what is necessary for the service to function.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To provide AI responses to your messages
  • To store your chat history so you can access it across sessions
  • To enforce subscription plans and daily message limits
  • To process payments and activate subscriptions
  • To send you important service notifications via Telegram

We do not use your conversations to train AI models. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Third-party services

Elyon AI uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google Gemini API — processes your messages for AI responses (Google's privacy policy applies to data sent to their API)
  • OpenAI / DeepSeek API — used for the free Core model
  • Telegram — for bot functionality and Telegram authentication
  • Google OAuth — for Google sign-in (optional)
  • CryptoBot / DonatePay — for payment processing
  • Vercel — for web hosting
  • Render — for backend hosting

5. Data retention

Your chat history is stored until you delete it or close your account. You can clear your chat history at any time through the chat interface.

If you have not used the service for 12 months, your data may be deleted.

6. Your rights

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your chat history

To exercise these rights, contact us via Telegram: @unkonyy

7. Security

We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes. Authentication tokens are time-limited and single-use.

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% secure. We recommend using Telegram login for the most secure authentication experience.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced via the Elyon AI Telegram channel @AI_Elyon. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
