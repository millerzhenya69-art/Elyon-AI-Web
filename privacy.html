Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Elyon AI

We built Elyon AI with privacy in mind. We collect the minimum data needed to provide the service, and we never sell your information to anyone.

1. What we collect

Account data

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. When you sign in with Telegram, we receive your Telegram ID, username, and first name. We store this to identify your account.

Conversation data

Your messages are sent to our AI backend to generate responses. We store recent conversation history to maintain context within a session. We do not use your conversations to train AI models.

Usage data

We log the number of messages sent per day to enforce daily limits. We do not log your IP address or device fingerprint.

2. What we don't collect

  • We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties
  • We don't store payment card details (payments handled via DonatePay / Telegram Stars)
  • We don't track your activity outside of Elyon AI

3. Third-party services

Elyon AI uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Vercel — hosting for this website
  • Render — backend server hosting
  • Google OAuth — optional sign-in method
  • Telegram — bot platform and optional sign-in
  • Google AI (Gemini) — AI model provider
  • DonatePay — card and SBP payments
  • CryptoBot — cryptocurrency payments

4. Data retention

Conversation history is retained for 30 days and then automatically deleted. Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Your rights

  • Access your data — contact us and we'll provide what we store
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your conversation history

6. Contact

For privacy questions: @testpythonunkony_bot on Telegram or through our bot @Elyon_by_unkony_bot.
