iPhone & iPad · Add to Home Screen
How to add Elyon to your iPhone home screen:

1. Open Safari on your iPhone (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Go to http://elyon-ai-web.vercel.app/ and tap the app to open it
3. Tap the Share button (box with arrow) at the bottom of the screen
4. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”
5. Tap “Add” in the top right corner

Elyon will appear on your home screen like a native app — with its own icon, no browser chrome, and full-screen mode.
